## 🕹️ About

Minecraft Dungeons II is an action RPG dungeon crawler set across the Minecraft universe, including the Overworld and unexplored lands. Fight a new threat, carve through hostile mobs, and build your hero with weapons, armor, artifacts, and legendary gear. This is a raw Windows pre-release build that surfaced ahead of the official September 29, 2026 release.

For dungeon-crawler fans who want accessible action, loot-driven builds, exploration, and chaotic co-op in a raw early-build package.

> ⚠️ **This is an unofficial leaked pre-release build.** The game is under active development: expect bugs, unfinished content, and balance changes.Archive is password protected (`2026`) to compatibility automated DMCA bots and hosting service scanners that would otherwise delete the files within hours.

## ✨ Features

- ⚔️ **Action RPG Combat** — mod through waves of mobs with melee weapons, ranged attacks, and active artifacts.
- 🗺️ **Overworld Adventures** — Explore interconnected Minecraft-themed regions, dungeons, and mysterious locations.
- 💎 **Build Your Hero** — Customize your playstyle with legendary weapons, armor, artifacts, and passive bonuses.
- 👹 **New Threats** — Face new enemies and a dangerous force spreading chaos across two worlds.
- 🤝 **Four-Player Co-op** — Play solo or team up with up to four players through local or online co-op.
